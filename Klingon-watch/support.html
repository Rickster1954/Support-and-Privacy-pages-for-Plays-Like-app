App Overview

Klingon Chrono is a Klingon time display and training app designed to help you learn Klingon numbers through visual recognition, audio playback, and interactive practice modes.

Features

  • Klingon digital and analog-style clock displays
  • Tap-to-hear pronunciation of time and numbers
  • Multiple voice profiles
  • Training modes including digit practice and speed drills
  • Customizable accent colors and display options

Privacy

Klingon Chrono does not collect or transmit personal data. All settings and usage remain on your device.
